Мои даты — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

Мои даты is an Android app for countdowns, elapsed days, local reminders and widgets. This policy explains what data is used, where it is stored and how you can manage it.

1. Data on your device

The app's local storage contains:

  • Event titles, dates, notes, counting modes, repeat rules, categories, appearance and reminder settings.
  • Your custom categories, chosen icons, language, theme, widget settings and copies of selected backgrounds.
  • First-use time, the number of event saves and the last review-request attempt, to limit how often a rating is suggested.

Core features work offline without an app account. Built-in illustrations, backgrounds and fonts do not need to be downloaded. The app has no cloud synchronization, advertising SDKs, in-app purchases or developer-operated analytics service. It does not use Google Play services (GMS) or Firebase.

2. Selected photos

Images are selected through Android's system file picker. The app opens the image you choose and crops it on your device. A cropped copy is saved in the app's internal storage for an event or widget background.

The original file is unchanged. Original photo metadata, such as location information, is not copied into the new image. The developer does not receive your selected photos, and the app does not upload them to a server.

3. Reminders and Android permissions

  • Reminders are calculated on the device and displayed as local notifications. Android 13 and later require notification permission for this. Declining does not prevent you from saving events or using widgets.
  • On Android 8–9, storage-write permission is requested only when you save a picture to the gallery. You can still share a picture with another app if you decline.
  • Internet access is included for the RuStore review service. Events are not synchronized over the internet. Android's system features also refresh widgets and restore reminder schedules after a restart.

Titles and dates may be visible in home-screen widgets and lock-screen notifications. Notification visibility can be controlled in Android settings.

4. Picture sharing, VK and the gallery

When you choose to share, the app creates a PNG containing the information shown on the event card and stores it in its cache. The app you choose receives temporary read access to that picture only. Receiving apps handle it under their own policies.

The VK button passes the picture to the installed VK client. You complete any sending or posting there. Мои даты has no VK SDK or VK sign-in, and does not upload the picture itself.

Saving to the gallery creates a separate copy in Pictures/Мои даты. Gallery copies and copies received by other apps are not removed when you delete an event or uninstall the app. Cached sharing files older than 24 hours may be deleted when you use sharing again.

5. Ratings and reviews in RuStore

The app uses RuStore Review 10.5.2. After some use, a RuStore rating form may appear. You choose whether to submit a rating and review. Settings also includes Rate in RuStore, which opens the app's page in RuStore or a browser.

To operate and diagnose the service, RuStore may process technical information about the device, app and SDK versions, connectivity, service identifiers and service events. We do not pass your event titles, dates, notes, photos or event database to the SDK.

Submitted ratings and reviews are handled by RuStore under its user privacy policy. Availability depends on connectivity, the RuStore client and its account. Participating in ratings is not required to use the core features of Мои даты.

6. Storage and deletion

You can delete events and custom categories in the app. Deleting an event removes its record. Saved background copies may remain in internal storage after a background is replaced or an event is deleted, as several widgets can use the same background.

Clearing app data in Android settings or fully uninstalling the app removes its local storage, including the database, settings and internal background copies. You manage gallery copies and copies sent to other apps separately. Automatic app-data backup is disabled; there are no built-in backup and restore features.

7. This web page

This policy is hosted on GitHub Pages. When you visit, GitHub logs the visitor's IP address for security. See GitHub Pages data collection and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

This applies to visiting the website. The page cannot access events or photos stored in the app. We add no analytics, advertising scripts, external fonts or our own cookies to this page.

8. Policy changes

Updated versions are published on this page with a new date. Before adding advertising, cloud features or other services, we will update the description of data processing and request consent where required.
